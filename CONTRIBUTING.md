# Contributing to VLA-MethodLab (independent laboratory)

VLA-MethodLab is a separately maintained research workspace, not an upstream simdjson submission channel.

- **The repository currently contains and distributes `engine/`**; treat it as a simdjson-derived, mixed-origin copy and retain its original licences. Keep its source synchronized with VectorPath-RVV for reproducible tests, and never state that it is absent from a published archive.
- Determine the actual origin, earlier grants, human authorship, third-party rights and first publication of every proposed original patch. Contributions and pull requests do not automatically transfer exclusive rights; obtain a written contribution agreement before merging.
- Do not describe benchmark values, scientific ideas, performance measurements, or public source architecture as exclusively owned just by placing them in a proprietary repository.
- For a new prospective original restricted patch, verify every criterion in `LICENSE-ORIGINAL-ADDITIONS.md`, add an exact path/patch entry to `PROVENANCE-ADDITIONS.md` and mark the designated portion before first publication.
- **No upstream simdjson integration permission is granted for eligible Covered Material.** Do not transmit such a patch to upstream for review or incorporation absent the actual rightsholder's signed authorization; existing Apache/MIT grants remain valid.
- AI-assisted work requires human review, independent test evidence, attribution and source provenance. Do not publish credentials or private .env settings.
- Maintain experimental logs and native benchmarks without retroactive alteration. Keep the embedded engine's licensing notices and all historical attributions.
