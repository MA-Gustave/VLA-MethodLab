This is an **independent RVV-VLA experimental repository**, not an official simdjson distribution. It contains **mixed-origin software**. No single new licence applies to everything in this repository.

| Original simdjson and inherited modifications already validly licensed | Apache-2.0 and/or MIT as applicable; preserved in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), [`LICENSE-MIT`](LICENSE-MIT), [`engine/LICENSE`](engine/LICENSE) and [`engine/LICENSE-MIT`](engine/LICENSE-MIT); check source-file and third-party notices. |

| **Precisely registered new original additions**, eligible for a distinct reservation | [`LICENSE-ORIGINAL-ADDITIONS.md`](LICENSE-ORIGINAL-ADDITIONS.md), subject to the strict eligibility test in that document and the register in [`PROVENANCE-ADDITIONS.md`](PROVENANCE-ADDITIONS.md). |
| New material without a verified, registered rights status | **No claim of a new enforceable restriction is made solely by this notice**. Verify the original licence, prior grants and publication history before relying on a reservation. |

The original Apache and MIT licence **texts and required source notices must not be deleted or edited**. The historical list of authors and contributors inside `engine/` is retained as attribution to inherited material, not as advertising or endorsement. Third-party licences remain applicable.

**Incorporation policy:** where a Covered Addition has been validly registered and is not subject to earlier applicable grants, no permission is granted for incorporating its protected implementation into another codebase without the owner's prior express written authorization. This policy is neutral and applies equally to all potential integrators.

**Distribution warning:** this snapshot contains no Git history, so ownership, earlier public distribution, contributions and pre-existing grants **have not been verified**. The presence of the root Apache/MIT licence files and the hosting platform's repository-licensing terms may independently establish permissions for code already published. A restrictive notice cannot undo those permissions. For genuinely new code needing strong restrictions, decide the scope and licence **before** publishing it, ideally in a clearly separated private module/repository and with legal advice.
